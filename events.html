Events

All our events are free. Bring a phone or a camera.

  • Every Saturday, 7 am: Photo walk. We meet at the main gate.
  • First Wednesday of the month, 4 pm: Workshop on light and shadow in Room 12.
  • Last Friday of the month, 3 pm: Show and tell. Bring your best photo of the month.

SPC Photography Club is a made-up club for a practice project.
