Welcome to the SPC Photography Club

We are students who love taking photos. Anyone can join. You do not need a big camera. A phone is fine.

Photo walks

Every Saturday morning we walk around the city and take photos together.

Workshops

Older members show you how to use light, frame a shot and edit a photo.

Show and tell

Once a month we each bring our best photo and talk about how we took it.

SPC Photography Club is a made-up club for a practice project.
